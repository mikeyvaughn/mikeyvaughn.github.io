Digital Leadership in SEIU-UHW

Workshops That Amplify
Member Voices.

As a digital leader within SEIU-UHW, Mikey is developing and facilitating social media workshops for political committee members, helping them bring their organizing and advocacy to Instagram and TikTok.

The workshops focus on sharing members’ work with colleagues at their hospitals, union pods, the executive board, and campaign teams. This extends his member development work into the digital spaces where workers connect and organize.
